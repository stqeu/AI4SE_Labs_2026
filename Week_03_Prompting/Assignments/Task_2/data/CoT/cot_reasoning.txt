## Conventions applied to every question

These rules came out of studying the schema and apply throughout the numbered reasoning:

- **Base join:** `restaurant AS t1 JOIN location AS t2 ON t1.id = t2.restaurant_id`. This puts both required output columns, `t2.house_number` and `t1.name`, in reach, in that order.
- **Filters:**
  - A city goes to `t2.city_name`.
  - A street goes to `t2.street_name`.
  - A food type goes to `t1.food_type`.
  - A name goes to `t1.name`.
  - A region ("in the bay area", "in the monterey", etc.) adds `JOIN geographic AS t3 ON t1.city_name = t3.city_name` and the filter `t3.region = …`.
- **Literal values:** every value is copied exactly as written, including oddities like `'st'`, `'ave.'`, `'n.shoreline blvd.'`, `'california'` and `'unknown'`.
- **Wording:**
  - **"best"** means the rating equals the MAX rating under the same filters, using a correlated subquery. This keeps ties and avoids an arbitrary `LIMIT 1`.
  - **"above X"** is a strict `t1.rating > X`. The parenthetical label, such as "(below average or better)", only describes the threshold and is ignored.
  - **"a restaurant", "some", "where can i…"** add no limit. All matches are returned.
- **Chatter:** everything about picnics, pancakes, umbrellas and stuffed animals is dropped.
